What the checklist covers.

  1. The caseback photo. No photo, no deal.
  2. Reference and serial. Do they match the model and its years?
  3. The dial print. The fastest way to spot a repainted dial.
  4. The lume. Dial and hands should have aged together.
  5. The case edges. Soft edges mean heavy polishing.
  6. The listing words. Three words that mean parts watch.
  7. The seller. Buy the seller, not the watch.
