Questions

What buyers ask before they click.

Where do the prices come from?

From real sold prices: eBay sold listings, auction results and collector sales. The fair range is the middle half of recent sales of good, original watches. Every page shows whether its price was verified against recent sales or is still an estimate being checked for the next edition.

I am not a collector. Is this for me?

Yes. Most readers want one or two good watches and do not want to be fooled. The book is written in plain English, with big type and no jargon you need to look up.

Is it only about Seiko?

No. Japan has the biggest chapter because that is where the best value is, but the book also covers Swiss, German, Soviet, American, British, French and Italian watches.

What format is it?

A PDF in Letter size, 175 pages. It works on any phone, tablet or computer, and prints well at home. Every watch name is a link, so on a screen you can jump straight to its page.

How do the updates reach me?

Each quarter the new edition is emailed to the address you used to buy. You can also download it again at any time from your purchase email.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a buyer's guide to help you pay a fair price. It does not tell you what to buy to make money, and it is not affiliated with any watch brand.
